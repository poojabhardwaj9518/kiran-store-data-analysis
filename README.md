# 🛒 Kiran Store Sales Analysis Dashboard (2025)

An interactive Excel-based data analysis project designed to evaluate sales performance, understand customer demographics, and identify high-growth channels for **Kiran Store**. This project transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objective
The primary goal of this analysis is to help Kiran Store optimize its sales strategy by answering key business questions:
* **Who are the top customers?** (Demographics, gender, and age-group analysis)
* **Where is the revenue coming from?** (Top-performing states and sales channels)
* **What is selling the most?** (Product category performance)
* **When do sales peak?** (Monthly sales and order trends)

---

## 🛠️ Key Features & Technical Skills

* **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Handled missing values, removed duplicates, and standardized data fields (e.g., age groups, states) using Excel formulas and filtering.
* **Data Modeling:** Built dynamic **Pivot Tables** to aggregate and summarize thousands of rows of sales data.
* **Interactive Visualization:** Designed a clean, user-friendly dashboard utilizing **Pivot Charts** (Bar charts, Line graphs, Pie charts).
* **Dynamic Filtering:** Implemented **Slicers** (for months, channels, and categories) allowing stakeholders to filter data on the fly.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* **Target Audience:** Women contribute significantly more to total sales/orders compared to men, making them a primary demographic for targeted marketing campaigns.
* **Top Channels:** E-commerce channels like Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra drive a major chunk of the order volume.
* **Geographic Hotspots:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh emerged as the top 3 states contributing to total revenue.
* **Peak Seasons:** Distinct monthly trends indicate specific periods of high purchasing activity, ideal for launching promotional offers.
